**Everyone deserves a chief of staff — your personal Jarvis.**

Mantrin (you'll also see it called Jarvis — that's the name it answers to,
and the name of the package inside) is a personal chief of staff you talk
to. Not a recorder and not a chat window: you say something once, and it
holds onto it and does something about it.

<Aside type="tip" title="Status: beta">
It runs a real day, every day — but it's young: Linux only for now, version
0.x, and things change without ceremony.
</Aside>

## What it does

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Remembers" icon="pencil">
		Your people, preferences, promises — memory that compounds instead of
		resetting every conversation.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Understands" icon="magnifier">
		Who matters, what's due, what you're actually after. Context, not just
		answers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Acts" icon="rocket">
		Sends the message, holds the slot, chases the loose end. Anything that
		touches other people, it asks you first.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
